## Exam strategy for this section

1. **Do not treat this as a rigid official syllabus:** use it as a broad, practical coverage checklist.
2. **Static + dynamic facts together:** learn the concept first, then update current figures, circulars, appointments and schemes.
3. **High priority:** RBI, banking structure, payments, financial institutions, government schemes, economy/budget, current affairs and banking-tech.
4. **Avoid low-value memorisation:** focus on facts that are commonly asked, recent, or connected to banking/financial institutions.
5. **Every lesson will include:** clear notes, exam traps, MCQs, explanations and a GitHub Markdown file.
